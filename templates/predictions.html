How these predictions are made

Plain-language method notes for each panel above.

  • Next-month forecast
    sklearn LinearRegression fitted to (month index, monthly total). Predicts the next calendar month. MAE / RMSE / R² are measured on the fit.
  • This-month projection
    Linear pacing: spent_so_far × days_in_month / days_elapsed.
  • Budget-overrun risk
    Projection = 60% pacing + 40% historical mean. Probability from sigmoid((projection − budget) / σ_history).
